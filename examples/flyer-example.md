# Flyer Example: Webinar on African Market Intelligence

## Brief Analysis

**Project** : Strategic Guidance Webinar Flyer
**Objective** : Generate registrations for webinar
**Audience** : African entrepreneurs, business owners, 25-50 years
**Format** : A4 (210x297mm)
**Timeline** : 2 weeks to delivery

---

## Creative Strategy

### Strategic Pillars

**Problem** : African entrepreneurs uncertain about market expansion strategy

**Opportunity** : Learn from experts with 15+ years in African markets

**Proof** : Afrique Boussole partnership with IFC, AfDB, and 500+ entrepreneurs

**Proposition** : Free webinar + expert guidance + community access

### Creative Angle Selected

**AUTHORITY** — Position as trusted expert

Visual language: Professional photography, institutional credibility, data-backed messaging

---

## Creative Direction

### Primary Message

"Strategic Navigation for African Markets"

**Subheading** : "Expert Guidance for Your Business Growth"

### Hook

Striking photograph of diverse African entrepreneurs in modern business environment, representing confidence and collaboration

### Color Palette

- Brand Blue (#013C87) — Headlines and primary elements
- Brand Green (#1D7742) — CTA button
- White (#FFFFFF) — Background
- Dark Navy (#0A2555) — Body text

---

## Imagery

**Concept** : Professional photograph of diverse African entrepreneurs in modern business setting

**Shot Type** : Group of 3-4 professionals, collaborative moment, modern office environment

**Lighting** : Natural professional lighting, warm white balance

**Color Grading** : Warm but professional, consistent with brand colors

**Details** :

- At least one female professional
- Diverse African backgrounds represented
- Contemporary business attire
- Modern office/co-working space
- Genuine expressions (not forced smiles)

---

## Call to Action

**Primary CTA** : "Register Now"

**Secondary URL** : afrique-boussole.com/webinar

**Button Style** :

- Background: Brand Green (#1D7742)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Padding: 14px x 28px
- Border radius: 4px
- Hover: Darker green (#164D3A)

✓ Message readable in 2 seconds? "Strategic Navigation for African Markets" — YES
