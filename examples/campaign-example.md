# Campaign Example: Q4 Market Entry Campaign

## Campaign Overview

**Campaign Name** : "Navigate African Markets: Q4 Growth Campaign"

**Duration** : October 1 - December 31, 2024 (13 weeks)

**Objective** : Generate 250 qualified leads for Strategic Advisory program

**Budget** : $50,000 USD

**Target Audience** : African entrepreneurs + international businesses seeking African expansion

---

## Campaign Strategy

### Core Message

"Strategic guidance transforms market opportunity into business growth"

### Campaign Concept

**"Navigation"** — Using compass and wayfinding metaphor to position Afrique Boussole as strategic guide

### Key Themes

1. **Access** — Unlock African market opportunities
2. **Clarity** — Strategic guidance reduces uncertainty
3. **Growth** — Evidence-based expansion strategy
4. **Community** — Network with 500+ entrepreneurs

---

## Creative Territories (4 Variations)

### Variation A: AUTHORITY

**Headline** : "Strategic Intelligence from Proven Experts"

**Visuals** : Professional office, data visualization, credentials

**Messaging** : "15 years in African markets. 500+ successful expansions."

**Tone** : Institutional, credible, professional

---

### Variation B: OPPORTUNITY

**Headline** : "Unlock Your African Market Potential"

**Visuals** : Open doors, growing trajectories, forward movement

**Messaging** : "Discover opportunities across 15 African markets"

**Tone** : Energetic, hopeful, inclusive

---

### Variation C: HUMAN IMPACT

**Headline** : "Stories of African Entrepreneurial Success"

**Visuals** : Real entrepreneurs, authentic moments, community

**Messaging** : "Meet the businesses transforming Africa"

**Tone** : Inspirational, relatable, community-focused

---

### Variation D: NAVIGATION

**Headline** : "Your Compass for Market Entry"

**Visuals** : Maps, directional elements, strategic pathways

**Messaging** : "Every market entry has a strategic roadmap"

**Tone** : Guiding, reassuring, strategic

---

- Hero image: Diverse African entrepreneurs collaborating in modern setting

## Format Adaptations

### Format 1: A4 Flyer (Master)

- 210x297mm
- 60% image, 40% text
- Main headline + subheading + CTA
- All 4 variations produced

### Format 2: A3 Poster

- 297x420mm
- Enlarged version of flyer
- Headline 96pt (readable at 1-3m)
- Minimal text, bold impact

### Format 3: Social 1:1 (Square)

- 1080x1080px
- Image cropped to focal point
- Headline overlay (28-48px)
- CTA in caption

### Format 4: Social 4:5 (Portrait)

- 1080x1350px
- Vertical emphasis
- Headline at top (54px)
- Body text removed (space-constrained)
- Large CTA button

### Format 5: Social 9:16 (Story)

- 1080x1920px
- Full vertical height
- Headline at top (56px)
- Image background
- CTA sticker at bottom

### Format 6: Email Header

- 1200x300px
- Landscape orientation
- Headline center-left (32px)
- CTA button right side
- Logo top-left

### Format 7: Web Banner

- 1920x1080px
- 16:9 landscape
- Split: 60% image, 40% text
- CTA button prominent
- Form integration possible

## Distribution Timeline

### Phase 1: Launch (Week 1-2, Oct 1-14)

**Channels** :
- Email announcement to existing database (500+ contacts)
- Social media: All platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok)
- Paid advertising: Google Ads, Meta Ads (small initial spend $5k)
- Website homepage feature
- Partner email blast (co-marketing)

**Formats** :
- Email header + CTA
- Social posts (all 4 variations introduced)
- Web banner
- Print: 500 flyers distributed

**Goal** : Brand awareness, audience familiarization with 4 concepts

---

### Phase 2: Amplify (Week 3-6, Oct 15-Nov 11)

**Focus** : Increase engagement, measure performance, identify winning variation

**Channels** :
- Increased paid spend ($20k) — A/B test variations
- LinkedIn Ads (targeting decision-makers)
- Instagram/Facebook Ads (targeting entrepreneurs)
- Email nurture sequence (3-email series)
- Content marketing: Blog posts aligned with each angle
- Webinar promotion

**Formats** :
- All formats, now with performance data informing allocation

**Goal** : Lead generation, 100+ sign-ups, identify top-performing variation (typically 1-2 variations outperform)

---

### Phase 3: Sustain (Week 7-10, Nov 12-Dec 9)

**Focus** : Scale winning variation, maintain engagement, retargeting

**Channels** :
- Increased organic social (daily posts)
- Retargeting ads ($15k) on website visitors
- Partner collaborations
- Community engagement (responding to comments, user-generated content)
- Email: Continued nurture sequence

**Formats** :
- Heavy emphasis on top-performing variation (70% of budget)
- Secondary variations at 30%

**Goal** : 150+ additional leads, momentum maintenance

---

### Phase 4: Close (Week 11-13, Dec 10-31)

**Focus** : Final push, urgency, year-end conversions

**Channels** :
- Holiday messaging (limited-time emphasis)
- Final email push
- Retargeting (last chance messaging)
- All paid channels continue

**Formats** :
- Winning variation + time-sensitive CTA

**Goal** : Final 50+ leads, close out quarter strong

## Success Criteria

**Campaign is successful if:**

1. ✓ Generate 250+ qualified leads (minimum)
2. ✓ Cost per lead ≤ $100
3. ✓ Email open rate ≥ 25%
4. ✓ Click-through rate ≥ 2.5%
5. ✓ Social engagement rate ≥ 3%
6. ✓ Website conversion rate ≥ 5%
7. ✓ Top variation outperforms by 50%+
8. ✓ All creatives pass QA standards
9. ✓ Consistent brand identity across all materials
10. ✓ Zero factual errors or misleading claims
