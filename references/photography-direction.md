# Afrique Boussole — Photography Direction

## Overview

Photography is a powerful tool in Afrique Boussole communications. It must be authentic, professional, and strategically chosen to support the brand narrative.

This direction ensures all photography—whether sourced from stock libraries or produced specifically—aligns with Afrique Boussole's visual identity and strategic message.

---

## 1. PHOTOGRAPHY PRINCIPLES

### Principle 1: Authenticity

**Photo must feel real, not staged.**

- Authentic expressions (not forced smiles)
- Natural environments (not artificial sets)
- Real work scenarios (not posed mockups)
- Genuine diversity (not token representation)
- Real moments (not overly produced)

**Avoid** :

- Overly polished, plastic-looking imagery
- Stock photo clichés (everyone smiling at laptop)
- Diversity as checkbox exercise
- Fake testimonials (stock photos of "customers")
- Artificial lighting that looks artificial

---

### Principle 2: Professional Quality

**Photo must be technically excellent.**

- Sharp focus
- Proper exposure
- Good composition
- Professional color grading
- High resolution (minimum 300dpi for print)
- Professional production value

**Avoid** :

- Blurry or out-of-focus photos
- Poor lighting
- Underexposed/overexposed
- Amateur composition
- Low resolution or pixelated
- Phone-quality photography (unless intentional artistic choice)

---

### Principle 3: Strategic Purpose

**Every photo must serve the message.**

- Photo supports headline, not distracts from it
- Image reinforces strategic angle
- Visual narrative aligns with copy
- Photo adds clarity or emotion, not confusion
- Image advances the audience understanding

**Avoid** :

- Generic images that could work for any brand
- Photos that compete with headline
- Images without clear relevance
- Decorative photos that don't add value
- Stock photos that are obviously stock

---

### Principle 4: African Contemporary

**Photo must represent modern Africa, not stereotypes.**

- Contemporary environments (modern offices, digital tools, tech)
- Modern aesthetics (clean, minimalist, professional)
- Diverse representation (gender, age, background, ability)
- Professional contexts (not rural/village settings unless strategic)
- Forward-looking imagery (not historical or nostalgic)

**Avoid** :

- "Safari" or nature imagery (unless specific strategy)
- Tribal or traditional imagery (unless culturally appropriate and strategic)
- Poverty or hardship as narrative
- Colonial-era aesthetics
- Clichéd "African sunset" imagery
- Homogenizing Africa as single narrative

## 4. PHOTOGRAPHY SPECIFICATIONS

### Resolution Requirements

**Digital Use** :

- Minimum 72 dpi (screen viewing)
- Minimum 1920px width (web banners)
- Minimum 1080x1080px (social media)
- Minimum 2400px width (recommended for future-proofing)

**Print Use** :

- Minimum 300 dpi
- A4 (210x297mm): 2480x3508px minimum
- Poster (A3 or larger): 3600px width minimum
- Outdoor advertising: 4000px+ width

---

### Color and Tone

**Color Palette** :

- Warm but not oversaturated
- Natural color grading (not artificial)
- Consistency with brand colors (blues and greens can be present)
- Avoid heavy color correction that looks fake

**Acceptable Tone** :

- Warm white balance (slightly golden)
- Cool white balance (professional, clean)
- Slightly muted vs. highly saturated (avoid extremes)

**Avoid** :

- Oversaturated colors (unrealistic)
- Desaturated to black-and-white (unless intentional artistic choice)
- Heavy filter overlays (Instagram-filter look)
- Artificial color grading that looks processed

---

### Composition Standards

**Framing** :

- Symmetrical or rule-of-thirds
- Clear focal point
- Appropriate negative space
- Professional cropping
- Safe margins for text overlay

**Depth** :

- Foreground, middle ground, background (when multiple subjects)
- Depth of field (sharp focus on subject, blurred background acceptable)
- Layering of elements
- Sense of dimension

**Angle and Perspective** :

- Eye-level (most common, relatable)
- Slightly elevated (authoritative, overview perspective)
- Avoid extreme angles unless strategic
- Avoid distortion unless intentional

---

### Lighting Standards

**Natural Light** :

- Soft, diffused light preferred
- Golden hour acceptable
- Avoid harsh shadows
- Even illumination
- No strong backlighting unless strategic

**Studio Light** :

- Professional three-point lighting
- Soft boxes and diffusion
- Even color temperature
- Shadow control
- No visible light stands or equipment

**Avoid** :

- Harsh midday sunlight
- Dark shadows covering subject
- Underexposed or overexposed areas
- Flickering or inconsistent lighting
- Artificial lighting that looks artificial

## 5. PHOTOGRAPHY SOURCING

### Stock Photography Guidelines

**Approved Stock Providers** :

- Unsplash (free, high-quality)
- Pexels (free, high-quality)
- Pixabay (free)
- Shutterstock (premium)
- Getty Images (premium)
- Envato Elements (premium)
- iStock (premium)

**Search Strategies** :

- Search for: "African entrepreneur," "business meeting," "modern office Africa"
- Avoid: "Africa," "African people," "stock photo"
- Look for: Authentic, professional, contemporary imagery
- Filter by: High resolution, diverse representation, professional quality

**Quality Control** :

- Download highest resolution available
- Verify licensing (especially for commercial use)
- Check image metadata
- Test readability with overlaid text
- Verify image is not obviously generic stock photo

---

### Original Photography

**When to Commission** :

- Testimonials or case studies (real clients)
- Founder/leadership portraits
- Branded campaign (premium positioning)
- Event documentation
- Team/culture imagery
- Custom concepts not found in stock

**Photographer Brief** :

```
OBJECTIVE: [What is the image communicating?]
SUBJECT MATTER: [People, environments, concepts?]
STYLE: [Authentic, professional, contemporary]
MOOD: [Energetic, calm, ambitious, collaborative?]
DIVERSITY: [Representation requirements?]
TECHNICAL SPECS: [Resolution, format, usage rights]
DELIVERABLES: [Color + B&W versions?]
TIMELINE: [When needed?]
```

---

### Rights and Licensing

**Always Verify** :

- Commercial use rights (not editorial-only)
- Geographic rights (worldwide preferred)
- Duration (perpetual vs. limited)
- Platform usage (web, print, social, etc.)
- Attribution requirements
- Exclusivity (if relevant)

**Attribution** :

- If required, credit photographer/source
- Place in small text, corner or metadata
- Example: "Photo by [Name] on Unsplash"

## 7. PHOTO EDITING AND RETOUCHING

### Acceptable Editing

- Color grading (consistent, subtle)
- Brightness/contrast adjustment
- Saturation adjustment (moderate)
- Sharpening (subtle)
- Removing distracting elements
- Straightening horizon
- Crop for composition

### Not Recommended

- Heavy Photoshop manipulation
- Removing people or subjects (use different photo instead)
- Extreme filter effects
- Blurring/distorting (unless intentional)
- Over-retouching (skin looks plastic)
- Changing environmental context

## 8. PHOTOGRAPHY MISTAKES TO AVOID

### Mistake 1: Generic Stock Photo

```
❌ WRONG: Random stock photo (could be for any company)
✅ RIGHT: Authentic, purposeful image that supports message
```

### Mistake 2: Low Quality or Blurry

```
❌ WRONG: Out-of-focus or pixelated image
✅ RIGHT: Sharp, professional-quality photo
```

### Mistake 3: Competing with Headline

```
❌ WRONG: Image is more interesting than headline
✅ RIGHT: Image supports and enhances headline
```

### Mistake 4: Stereotypical Representation

```
❌ WRONG: Photo relies on clichés or stereotypes
✅ RIGHT: Authentic, contemporary representation
```

### Mistake 5: Poor Lighting

```
❌ WRONG: Underexposed, overexposed, or harsh shadows
✅ RIGHT: Professional, even lighting
```

### Mistake 6: Text Unreadable on Image

```
❌ WRONG: White text on light-colored photo (no contrast)
✅ RIGHT: Text overlay with darkened background or contrasting color
```
