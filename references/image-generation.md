# Afrique Boussole — Image Generation Guide

## Overview

When photography cannot be sourced or commissioned, AI image generation tools (DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion) can produce visuals that align with Afrique Boussole's aesthetic.

This guide ensures generated images are high-quality, on-brand, and indistinguishable from professional photography.

---

## 1. IMAGE GENERATION PRINCIPLES

### Principle 1: Prompts Must Be Highly Specific

Vague prompts produce generic results. Detailed prompts produce strategic results.

**Weak Prompt** :
```
"African business people"
```

**Strong Prompt** :
```
"Professional African entrepreneur, female, 30s, in modern minimalist office, 
working at standing desk, warm natural light from large windows, 
professional business casual attire, confident expression, 
high-resolution photography, shallow depth of field, golden hour lighting, 
shot with professional DSLR, bright and aspirational mood"
```

---

### Principle 2: Separate Image Generation from Composition

Don't ask the image generator to include text, complex layouts, or specific positioning.

**Wrong** :
```
"Create a flyer with headline 'Navigate African Markets' and CTA button"
```

**Right** :
```
"Generate base image of: [detailed description]"
Then: Add text, CTA, and design elements separately
```

---

### Principle 3: Use Reference and Iteration

First generation rarely produces final result. Iterate:

1. Generate initial concept
2. Evaluate against requirements
3. Refine prompt based on output
4. Generate variations
5. Select best option
6. Use as foundation for design

## 2. PROMPT STRUCTURE

### Components of a Strong Image Prompt

```
[SUBJECT] + [SETTING] + [ACTION/POSE] + [STYLE] + [TECHNICAL] + [MOOD]
```

### Component Details

#### SUBJECT

**Describe the main element precisely:**

- "Professional African entrepreneur, female, 30s, East African features"
- "Diverse team of 4 people, mixed ages and genders, African heritage"
- "Modern office building with glass facade, contemporary architecture"
- "African map in contemporary minimalist design"

#### SETTING

**Describe the environment:**

- "Inside modern open-plan office with natural light"
- "Contemporary co-working space, bright and airy"
- "Professional conference room, glass table, contemporary furniture"
- "Outdoor urban African city setting, modern skyline"

#### ACTION/POSE

**Describe what's happening:**

- "Standing confidently at standing desk, looking at monitor"
- "Collaborating around table, pointing at digital dashboard"
- "In presentation mode, engaged with audience"
- "Shaking hands professionally"

#### STYLE

**Describe visual aesthetic:**

- "Professional photography style"
- "Shot with professional DSLR camera"
- "High-resolution, magazine-quality photography"
- "Contemporary photojournalism style"
- "Cinematic photography"

#### TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

**Specify technical parameters:**

- "Shot with 50mm lens, shallow depth of field"
- "f/2.8 aperture, professional color grading"
- "Golden hour natural light, warm color temperature"
- "High resolution, sharp focus, soft background blur"
- "Soft diffused lighting, no harsh shadows"

#### MOOD

**Describe emotional tone:**

- "Confident, professional, aspirational"
- "Positive energy, collaborative spirit"
- "Forward-thinking, innovative"
- "Trustworthy, institutional"
- "Dynamic, energetic"

## 4. SPECIFIC GUIDANCE BY SUBJECT

### People Photography

**Best Practices** :

- Specify ethnicity and African origin (East, West, Southern, North)
- Describe age range (not exact age, which can be inaccurate)
- Specify gender clearly
- Describe clothing style (business casual, formal, creative)
- Describe expression and body language
- Mention professional context

**Example** :

```
Professional African woman, South African heritage, 28-35 years, 
serious and focused expression, wearing black blazer over professional dress, 
sitting at modern desk with laptop, 
warm natural office lighting, 
shot from slight 3/4 angle, 
shallow depth of field, 
professional business photography
```

---

### Environment and Setting

**Best Practices** :

- Describe architectural style (modern, contemporary, minimalist)
- Specify location context (African country or city, if relevant)
- Describe lighting condition
- Mention technology/contemporary elements
- Describe mood and atmosphere

**Example** :

```
Modern open-plan office in contemporary African city, 
high ceilings with skylights providing natural light, 
mix of individual workstations and collaborative spaces, 
glass partitions and natural wood elements, 
diverse professionals visible working, 
daylight streaming through large windows, 
professional corporate environment, 
contemporary and forward-thinking atmosphere
```

---

### Data and Strategic Visuals

**Best Practices** :

- Describe data representation clearly
- Specify map or geographic elements
- Mention geometric or technological aesthetic
- Describe integration with professional context
- Note clean, minimalist style

**Example** :

```
Digital map of Africa with illuminated route lines and connection nodes, 
showing major cities and trade corridors, 
3D perspective with subtle depth, 
glowing blue and green lines representing connectivity, 
contemporary tech aesthetic, 
displayed on high-resolution monitor in professional setting, 
professional visualization, 
high resolution, strategic and intelligent mood
```

## 5. TOOL-SPECIFIC GUIDANCE

### DALL-E 3

**Strengths** : 
- Text comprehension, nuanced descriptions
- Good at understanding complex requirements
- Produces consistent quality

**Best For** : 
- Complex, detailed concepts
- Text-heavy descriptions
- Nuanced emotional requirements

**Prompt Style** : 
- Conversational, detailed
- Can be longer and more descriptive
- DALL-E understands narrative

---

### Midjourney

**Strengths** :
- High aesthetic quality, artistic control
- Excellent for photorealism
- Fast iteration

**Best For** :
- Photography-realistic images
- Aesthetic refinement
- Rapid prototyping

**Prompt Style** :
- Concise with specific keywords
- Use "--ar 16:9" for aspect ratio
- Use "--style raw" or "--niji" for specific aesthetics
- Use "--no [element]" to exclude unwanted elements

**Example Midjourney Prompt** :
```
African entrepreneur woman, 30s, modern office, standing at desk, 
confident expression, professional photography, golden light, 
shallow depth of field, magazine quality --ar 16:9 --no logo --style raw
```

---

### Stable Diffusion

**Strengths** :
- Open source, customizable
- Good control via parameters
- Cost-effective at scale

**Best For** :
- Batch generation
- Specific stylistic control
- Custom model fine-tuning

**Prompt Style** :
- Keyword-based, structured
- Include negative prompts explicitly
- Specify model and parameters
- Use tags for aesthetic style

**Example SD Prompt** :
```
Positive: professional african entrepreneur woman 30s modern office standing desk 
golden light DSLR photography shallow depth of field magazine quality
Negative: low quality, blurry, oversaturated, plastic, fake, cliche, logo, text
Model: Realistic Vision v5
CFG: 7.5
```

## 6. IMAGE GENERATION WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Define Requirements

```
What do I need the image to show?
Who is the subject?
What setting?
What mood?
What will it be used for?
```

### Step 2: Write Detailed Prompt

Use the prompt structure: [SUBJECT] + [SETTING] + [ACTION] + [STYLE] + [TECHNICAL] + [MOOD]

### Step 3: Generate Variations

Create 3-5 variations with different prompt nuances.

### Step 4: Evaluate Against Requirements

- Does it match the brief?
- Is quality high enough?
- Is it on-brand?
- Is it better than available stock photography?

### Step 5: Iterate if Needed

Refine prompt and regenerate if needed. Usually 1-3 iterations sufficient.

### Step 6: Process and Prepare

- Export at high resolution (2400px minimum)
- Adjust color/tone if needed (subtle)
- Ensure licensing allows commercial use
- Add to design composition

## 7. QUALITY STANDARDS FOR GENERATED IMAGES

### Resolution

- Minimum 2400px on longest side (AI generators often produce 1024-2048px)
- Upscale if necessary using AI upscaling (Topaz Gigapixel, Real-ESRGAN)
- Verify upscaling preserves quality and doesn't create artifacts

### Realism

- Image should be indistinguishable from professional photography
- No obvious "AI artifacts" (weird hands, odd textures, distortion)
- Lighting should be natural and believable
- Proportions should be realistic

### Brand Alignment

- Colors should align with brand palette
- Aesthetic should feel professional and contemporary
- Image should not look generic or templated
- Should feel authentically Afrique Boussole

### Composition

- Focal point should be clear
- Composition should follow design principles (rule of thirds, balance, etc.)
- Background should support main subject
- Negative space should be appropriate

## 8. WHEN NOT TO USE AI IMAGE GENERATION

**Avoid AI generation when:**

- **Real testimonials are needed** (use photography of actual client/person)
- **Specific branded elements are needed** (logo, product, specific person)
- **Trust/authenticity is paramount** (CEO portrait, team photo)
- **Professional photography exists** (use original)
- **Stock photography is sufficient** (save generation time)

**Use AI generation when:**

- No suitable photography exists
- Concept is too specific for stock
- Timeline requires rapid turnaround
- Budget doesn't allow professional photography
- Illustrative rather than documentary aesthetic is appropriate

## 9. UPSCALING AND ENHANCEMENT

### AI Upscaling Tools

**Topaz Gigapixel AI** (Paid, excellent quality)
- Enlarges 2x to 6x without quality loss
- Excellent for photos and photorealistic images

**Real-ESRGAN** (Free, open-source)
- 2x to 4x upscaling
- Good quality, sometimes over-sharpens

**Upscayl** (Free, user-friendly)
- Integrates Real-ESRGAN
- Simple interface

### When to Upscale

- Generated image is 1024px or 1536px, need 2400px+
- Upscale to required size before integration
- Always verify upscaled result doesn't look artificial

## 11. PROMPTING MISTAKES TO AVOID

### Mistake 1: Vague Prompts

```
❌ WRONG: "African business"
✅ RIGHT: "Professional African entrepreneur woman, 35, West African, 
in modern office, confident expression, professional photography"
```

### Mistake 2: Asking for Text in Image

```
❌ WRONG: "Image with headline 'Navigate African Markets' and logo"
✅ RIGHT: Generate image, add text separately in design software
```

### Mistake 3: Unspecific Subject

```
❌ WRONG: "People working"
✅ RIGHT: "Diverse team of 5, ages 25-50, mixed gender, 
in collaborative meeting around glass table"
```

### Mistake 4: Overly Complex Requests

```
❌ WRONG: "African map with routes, data visualization, 
office background, people pointing, golden light..."
✅ RIGHT: Generate simpler base image, composite elements in design software
```

### Mistake 5: Ignoring Quality Standards

```
❌ WRONG: Accepting low-quality, artifact-ridden output
✅ RIGHT: Regenerate or iterate until quality is professional
```

## 12. LICENSING AND COMMERCIAL USE

### Always Verify

- Tool's terms of service allow commercial use
- Usage rights are perpetual and worldwide
- No attribution required (or attribution is acceptable)
- Images can be modified/edited
- AI-generated images are not reserved for exclusive use

### Common Licensing

**DALL-E** : Commercial use allowed, no attribution required

**Midjourney** : Commercial use allowed (with paid subscription)

**Stable Diffusion** : Depends on model; most allow commercial use
