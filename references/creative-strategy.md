# Afrique Boussole — Creative Strategy Framework

## Overview

Ce framework transforme un objectif marketing en concept créatif cohérent et visuellement exécutable.

Chaque création pour Afrique Boussole doit:

1. Répondre à un objectif clair
2. Cibler une audience spécifique
3. Proposer un angle créatif défensible
4. Communiquer une valeur ou bénéfice
5. Inclure un appel à l'action

## 5. CREATIVE BRIEF ANALYSIS PROCESS

### Step 1: Extract the Core

Read the brief and identify:

1. **Objective** : What outcome do we want?
2. **Audience** : Who are we reaching?
3. **Message** : What's the core idea?
4. **CTA** : What should they do next?

### Step 2: Identify the Angle

Which of the 6 angles best serves this objective?

- **AUTHORITY** if: positioning as trusted expert, institutional communication
- **OPPORTUNITY** if: promoting access, growth, new possibilities
- **HUMAN IMPACT** if: storytelling, transformation, community focus
- **NAVIGATION** if: strategic guidance, direction, clarity
- **DATA & EXPERTISE** if: research, insights, evidence-based messaging
- **EDUCATION** if: learning, capability building, how-to content

Can multiple angles work? Yes. But choose PRIMARY angle.

### Step 3: Define the Hook

*What stops the audience and makes them pay attention?*

- Surprising insight?
- Emotional trigger?
- Visual contrast?
- Relevant stat or fact?
- Question that resonates?
- Visual metaphor?
- Bold statement?
- Authentic story?

### Step 4: Articulate the Value

*Why should they care?*

- Problem solved?
- Opportunity unlocked?
- Knowledge gained?
- Connection made?
- Results promised?
- Impact contributed?
- Status/positioning?

### Step 5: Craft the CTA

*What's the next step?*

- Visit website
- Download resource
- Register / RSVP
- Contact us
- Schedule meeting
- Apply / Submit
- Join community
- Learn more
- Watch video
- Share with network

**CTA must be:**

- Clear and specific
- Easy to execute
- Appropriate to audience
- Aligned with objective
- Visually prominent

## 6. VALIDATION CHECKLIST

Before proceeding to creative concept:

- [ ] Objective is clear and measurable
- [ ] Audience is defined and understood
- [ ] Message is single and focused
- [ ] Angle (AUTHORITY / OPPORTUNITY / HUMAN / NAVIGATION / DATA / EDUCATION) is selected
- [ ] Hook is attention-grabbing and relevant
- [ ] Value proposition is clear
- [ ] CTA is specific and actionable
- [ ] Message feels authentic to Afrique Boussole
- [ ] Message addresses the stated objective
- [ ] We're not making assumptions about audience needs
- [ ] Constraints (format, size, timeline) are documented

## 7. COMMON STRATEGIC MISTAKES TO AVOID

**Mistake 1: Unclear Objective**

*Problem* : "Make a flyer that looks good"

*Solution* : "Generate 30 qualified leads for our Market Intelligence program"

**Mistake 2: Vague Audience**

*Problem* : "Everyone who uses social media"

*Solution* : "African entrepreneurs with 2-10 years experience seeking market expansion guidance"

**Mistake 3: Multiple Messages**

*Problem* : "We do strategy, training, and consulting"

*Solution* : Pick ONE message for this specific creative

**Mistake 4: No Clear CTA**

*Problem* : "Just make it look professional"

*Solution* : "Drive sign-ups for the webinar on LinkedIn"

**Mistake 5: Ignoring Angle**

*Problem* : Using generic, non-specific visual language

*Solution* : Pick an angle and commit to its visual language

**Mistake 6: Missing Proof**

*Problem* : Making claims without evidence

*Solution* : Include data, testimonials, or credibility signals

## 8. MULTI-VARIATION STRATEGY

When creating multiple variations of the same campaign:

### Concept Variations

Create 3-4 variations using DIFFERENT angles, all supporting the same objective.

**Example Campaign** : "Join Our Entrepreneurs Network"

- **Variation A (AUTHORITY)** : "Trusted Guidance for Market Entry"
- **Variation B (OPPORTUNITY)** : "Unlock Your African Market Potential"
- **Variation C (HUMAN IMPACT)** : "Success Stories from Real Entrepreneurs"
- **Variation D (NAVIGATION)** : "Your Strategic Roadmap Starts Here"

Each variation:

- Has different visual approach
- Uses different hook
- Emphasizes different value
- Feels distinct but cohesive
- Maintains brand identity

### Execution Variations

Once concept is selected, create variations for:

- Different formats (1:1 social, 16:9 banner, A4 print)
- Different audiences (corporate vs. startup)
- Different platforms (LinkedIn vs. Instagram)
- Different CTAs (Sign up vs. Learn more)
- Different seasonality (Launch vs. Evergreen)
