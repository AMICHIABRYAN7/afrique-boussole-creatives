# Afrique Boussole — Platform Guidelines

## Overview

Each social platform and medium has unique constraints, audience behaviors, and content expectations.

This guide optimizes Afrique Boussole creatives for each platform while maintaining brand consistency.

---

## 1. INSTAGRAM

### Platform Characteristics

- **Audience** : 25-45 year-old professionals, entrepreneurs, business decision-makers
- **Behavior** : Visual browsing, 3-5 second average view time
- **Content Type** : Photography, graphics, video
- **Algorithm** : Engagement-based (likes, comments, shares, saves)

### Content Strategy

**Post Type Priority** :

1. Carousel posts (1-3 slides) — High engagement
2. Reels (video) — Algorithm-favored
3. Feed photos/graphics — Standard
4. Stories (ephemeral) — Daily engagement

### Creative Specifications

**Feed Post (1:1 or 4:5)** :

- Bold, eye-catching primary image
- Minimal text overlay (viewers can read caption)
- High contrast for visibility in feed
- Headline readable at 200px width
- CTA can be in caption (not image)

**Reels** :

- 9:16 or 4:5 format (vertical)
- Duration 15-90 seconds
- Hook in first 1 second (or scroll passes)
- Text overlay bold and large (readable while scrolling)
- CTA in caption or end screen

**Carousel** :

- Slide 1 : Hook (most important)
- Slides 2-3 : Supporting information
- Each slide should work standalone
- Consistent aesthetic across slides
- Final slide : CTA

**Stories** :

- 9:16 format
- 1-3 seconds per slide (or longer if educational)
- Text stickers, buttons, interactive elements
- CTA buttons (Swipe Up if 10k followers, Link Sticker otherwise)
- Brand consistency across story series

### Text Optimization

- **Caption** : 125-150 characters before "more" (optimize for mobile)
- **Hashtags** : 5-10 relevant hashtags (post-caption or first comment)
- **Mentions** : Tag relevant accounts (partnerships, features)
- **Tone** : Professional yet approachable, conversational

### Audience Targeting

- Decision-makers and entrepreneurs
- Use language: "Strategy," "Growth," "Opportunity," "Leadership"
- Post timing: 9 AM or 5-6 PM (professional browsing times)
- Consistency: 3-4 posts per week

## 2. LINKEDIN

### Platform Characteristics

- **Audience** : 35-55 year-old executives, business owners, institutional buyers
- **Behavior** : Professional networking, business decision-making focus
- **Content Type** : Text, professional photography, thought leadership
- **Algorithm** : Engagement-based (shares, comments, clicks)

### Content Strategy

**Priority Content Types** :

1. Thought leadership articles / insights
2. Professional photography + compelling copy
3. Company/institutional announcements
4. Webinar/event promotion
5. Case studies and results

### Creative Specifications

**LinkedIn Feed Post** :

- **Format** : 1:1 or landscape (16:9 acceptable)
- **Visual** : Professional photography, data visualization, or corporate graphic
- **Headline** : 100-120 characters, punchy
- **Copy** : 150-300 words (LinkedIn rewards length for engagement)
- **CTA** : "Learn more," "Connect," "Comment," "Share"

**Professional Photography Requirements** :

- Formal business attire
- Corporate/institutional settings
- High production quality
- Diverse representation
- Authentic (not stock photo cliché)

**Typography** :

- Headline: Large, professional
- Body: Clean, readable, 14-16px
- CTA: Clear and professional

### Document/Carousel Specifications

**LinkedIn Document (PDF-style)** :

- Pages 1-5 recommended (users tire after 5 pages)
- Clean, professional design
- Readable on mobile
- Title page with clear headline
- Conclusion with CTA

**Carousel** :

- 3-5 slides maximum
- Infographic style or step-by-step
- Each slide should be clear standalone
- Professional aesthetic throughout

### Audience Addressing

- Use formal language: "Strategic partners," "Executive leadership," "Decision-makers"
- Reference specific industries: "Financial Services," "Development Organizations," "Tech Startups"
- Professional tone, but not stuffy
- Credibility is paramount

### Posting Strategy

- Frequency: 2-3 times per week
- Best times: Tuesday-Thursday, 8 AM - 2 PM
- Encourage engagement: Ask questions, request shares
- Respond to comments promptly (boosts algorithm)

## 3. FACEBOOK

### Platform Characteristics

- **Audience** : 45-65 year-old decision-makers, organizations, community builders
- **Behavior** : News feed browsing, group participation
- **Content Type** : Mixed (photo, video, text, articles)
- **Algorithm** : Engagement-based, family-friendly preference

### Creative Specifications

**Feed Post** :

- **Format** : 1.91:1 (wide), 4:5 (portrait), or 1:1 (square)
- **Visual** : Professional but approachable (less formal than LinkedIn)
- **Text** : 50-80 characters in image (audience is older, prefers larger text)
- **Copy** : 100-200 words, conversational tone
- **CTA** : "Learn More," "Register," "Donate," "Share"

**Audience Network** :

- Institutional messaging (nonprofits, government, organizations)
- Community announcements
- Events and webinars
- Educational content

### Video Strategy

- Autoplay video with captions (25-60 seconds)
- Captions mandatory (many watch without sound)
- Hook in first 2 seconds
- CTA in last 5 seconds

### Targeting

- Organizations and institutions
- Formal events and announcements
- Community engagement
- Accessibility emphasis (captions, audio descriptions)

## 4. TikTok

### Platform Characteristics

- **Audience** : 18-35 year-old younger professionals, entrepreneurs, digital natives
- **Behavior** : Short-form video scrolling, rapid-fire consumption
- **Content Type** : Primarily video, high energy
- **Algorithm** : Engagement and watch-time based

### Creative Specifications

**Video Specs** :

- **Format** : 9:16 (full vertical)
- **Duration** : 15-60 seconds (30 seconds optimal)
- **Hook** : First 1-2 seconds critical
- **Captions** : Mandatory (on-screen text overlays)
- **Sound** : Trending audio, original audio, voiceover

**Content Ideas for B2B** :

- Quick tips (15 seconds)
- Behind-the-scenes
- "Day in the life" of professional
- Educational quick-hits
- Founder/CEO personality
- Industry hot takes
- Trend participation (adapted to brand)

### Tone and Style

- Authentic and less polished than other platforms
- Personality-driven (founder, team member)
- Can be humorous or serious
- Energetic pacing
- Gen-Z friendly without being inappropriate

### Posting Strategy

- Frequency: 2-4 times per week
- Post when audience is most active (analytics reveal)
- Engage with comments and duets/stitches
- Don't overly brand (too corporate fails on TikTok)

## 5. YOUTUBE

### Platform Characteristics

- **Audience** : All ages, but 25-54 dominant for B2B
- **Behavior** : Long-form content consumption, search-based discovery
- **Content Type** : Video (primarily)
- **Algorithm** : Watch time, click-through rate, engagement

### Creative Specifications

**Thumbnail** :

- 1280x720px
- Bold, high-contrast image
- Headline text (24-40pt, readable at small size)
- Brand color or logo (subtle)
- Professional photography or graphic
- Text should not obscure primary image

**Video Format** :

- 16:9 (horizontal)
- 1080p minimum resolution (1440p or 4K preferred)
- 30fps or 60fps frame rate
- Professional audio (clear, consistent level)
- Captions or subtitles (at least in English, French)

**Title** :

- 60 characters maximum (truncated after)
- Keyword-optimized
- Compelling and specific
- Example: "How to Navigate African Markets: Strategic Guide for Entrepreneurs"

**Description** :

- 150-300 characters visible (first paragraph crucial)
- Include timestamp chapters (for longer videos)
- Links to resources, website, social
- Keyword-rich but natural language

**Video Types for B2B** :

1. **Webinar Recording** (30-60 min)
   - Edited for YouTube (remove technical issues)
   - Chapters/timestamps for navigation
   - Call-to-action in intro and outro
   - Promotional graphic in corner or intro

2. **Tutorial/How-To** (5-15 min)
   - Clear, step-by-step progression
   - On-screen text and graphics for clarity
   - B-roll and visual demonstrations
   - CTA at end ("Subscribe," "Learn more")

3. **Case Study/Success Story** (8-12 min)
   - Client testimonial
   - Before/after visuals
   - Results/metrics
   - CTA and contact information

4. **Thought Leadership** (10-20 min)
   - Executive or expert speaking
   - Slides or graphics supporting points
   - Professional production quality
   - Clear headline for each section

### Thumbnail and Branding

- Consistent thumbnail style (same colors, layouts across series)
- Brand logo small but visible (bottom right acceptable)
- Face visibility increases click-through (if applicable)
- Text contrast critical (readable at 100px width)

## 6. EMAIL MARKETING

### Creative Specifications

**Email Width** :

- Desktop: 600px content width (900px total with margins)
- Mobile: 100% width (responsive)
- Test across Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail

**Email Template Zones** :

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ HEADER (Logo, subject line visual)  │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ HERO IMAGE (1:1 or 16:9)            │
│ HEADLINE (H1, white or branded)     │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ BODY TEXT (14px, line height 1.6)   │
│                                     │
│ SECTION 1 (H2, supporting text)     │
│                                     │
│ CTA BUTTON (Brand Green, 16px)      │
│                                     │
│ SECTION 2 (optional)                │
│                                     │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ FOOTER (Links, social, address)     │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Subject Line** :

- 45-50 characters (optimal)
- Benefit-driven or curiosity-driven
- No ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!
- Personalization tags (Name, Company) if available

**Preview Text** :

- 85-100 characters visible in inbox
- Expand on subject line value proposition
- Not "View in browser"

**Body Copy** :

- Font: Inter (or fallback Sans-serif)
- Size: 14px minimum
- Line height: 1.6
- Color: Dark Navy (#0A2555) on White
- Ample spacing between sections

**CTA Button** :

- Background: Brand Green (#1D7742)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Padding: 12px 24px minimum
- Font: 14-16px, Weight 600
- Border radius: 4px
- Hover state: Darker Green (#164D3A)

## 7. OUT-OF-HOME (OOH) ADVERTISING

### Billboard/Poster Advertising

**Spec Requirements** :

- Minimum 300dpi for print (2400px minimum for 8"x10" print)
- CMYK color space
- Safe margins: 1/2" on all sides (bleed area)
- Font minimum 36pt (for 12 feet viewing distance)

**Design Principles** :

- **Simple** : 3-5 words maximum
- **Bold** : High contrast, vibrant colors
- **Readable** : No small text, large headlines
- **Memorable** : Single strong message
- **Brand** : Logo visible, colors consistent

**Copy Formula** :

```
HEADLINE (largest)
(single most important message)

SUPPORTING TEXT (medium)
(brief explanation)

LOGO + CTA
(where to find more info: website, phone)
```

## 8. PRINT MARKETING

### Business Cards

**Size** : 3.5" x 2" (standard)
**Color** : Full color CMYK
**Finish** : Matte or glossy
**Paper** : 300gsm cardstock minimum

---

### Brochures

**Tri-fold** :
- Folded: 3.67" x 8.5"
- Unfolded: 11" x 8.5"
- Front cover (what hook to attention)
- Inside spread (detailed information)
- Back cover (CTA, contact)

**Design** :
- Professional photography
- Clear hierarchy
- Ample white space
- High contrast text
- Professional color printing

---

### Direct Mail

**Postcard** :
- 4" x 6" minimum
- CMYK color, 300dpi
- Message on front (hook + CTA)
- Address block on back

**Letter** :
- 8.5" x 11" standard
- Letterhead at top
- Professional body text (11pt minimum)
- Signature or team photo (builds trust)
