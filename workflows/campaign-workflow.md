# Campaign Workflow

## Overview

A campaign is a coordinated series of creatives across multiple formats and platforms, all supporting a single strategic objective.

---

## Process

1. **Define Campaign Objective** → Lead gen? Awareness? Launch? Event promotion?
2. **Identify Audience** → Primary and secondary audiences
3. **Develop Campaign Concept** → Central creative idea/visual
4. **Define Key Message** → Single message across all materials
5. **Choose Creative Angles** → 3-4 variations of the concept
6. **Design Master Visual** → Iconic image/graphic for campaign
7. **Create Variations** → Concept A (AUTHORITY), B (OPPORTUNITY), C (HUMAN), D (NAVIGATION)
8. **Adapt Formats** → Flyer, poster, social (1:1, 4:5, 9:16), email, web banner
9. **Develop Timeline** → Launch date, weekly schedule, duration
10. **Build Asset Library** → All versions organized and documented
11. **Launch** → Deploy across channels simultaneously
12. **Monitor** → Track performance, engagement, conversions
13. **Optimize** → Adjust based on performance data
14. **Conclude** → Measure total campaign results

---

## Campaign Structure

### Phase 1: Strategy & Planning

- Campaign objective and KPIs
- Target audience profile
- Key message and positioning
- Creative territories (the 4 angles)
- Timeline and milestones
- Budget allocation

### Phase 2: Creative Development

- Master visual (hero image/graphic)
- Concept variations (4 distinct approaches)
- Copy library (headlines, sub-headlines, CTAs)
- Visual style guide (colors, treatment, mood)

### Phase 3: Asset Production

- Primary format (e.g., A4 flyer)
- Format adaptations (posters, social, web, email)
- All platform-specific versions
- Mockups and previews
- Quality assurance

### Phase 4: Launch & Execution

- Scheduling across platforms
- Social media posting calendar
- Email send dates/times
- Print distribution/posting
- PR/announcement timing
- Influencer/partner coordination

### Phase 5: Measurement

- Engagement metrics
- Conversion metrics
- Audience reach
- Cost per acquisition
- ROI calculations
- Learnings documentation

## Multi-Format Adaptation

**Starting Point** : Master flyer (A4 or similar)

**Derived Formats** :

- Poster (A3, enlarged version)
- Social 1:1 (square, cropped and recomposed)
- Social 4:5 (portrait, recomposed)
- Social 9:16 (story, minimal text)
- Email header (landscape, optimized)
- Web banner (1920x1080, landscape)

**Principle** : Recompose, don't shrink. Each format should look intentional, not derivative.

---

## Multi-Variation Strategy

**Variation A: AUTHORITY**

- Visual: Professional photography, data, credentials
- Message: "Trusted guidance," "Expert strategy"
- Tone: Formal, institutional

**Variation B: OPPORTUNITY**

- Visual: Forward-looking, open, aspirational
- Message: "Unlock potential," "Discover possibilities"
- Tone: Energetic, positive

**Variation C: HUMAN IMPACT**

- Visual: Real people, authentic moments
- Message: "Stories of transformation," "Community-driven"
- Tone: Emotional, relatable

**Variation D: NAVIGATION**

- Visual: Compass, maps, directional
- Message: "Find your direction," "Strategic clarity"
- Tone: Reassuring, guiding

## Timeline Example (4-Week Campaign)

**Week 1: Planning & Creative**
- Brief finalized
- Concept developed
- Variations approved

**Week 2: Asset Production**
- All creatives designed and approved
- Platform versions prepared
- QA completed

**Week 3: Launch**
- Day 1: Email announcement
- Day 2: Social media ramp-up
- Day 3-4: Paid advertising begins
- Week continues: Daily social engagement

**Week 4: Sustain & Measure**
- Ongoing social engagement
- Ongoing paid advertising
- Email retargeting
- Weekly performance review
- Campaign optimization

## Performance Metrics

**Engagement** :
- Impressions
- Reach
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Click-through rate

**Conversion** :
- Clicks from ads
- Form submissions
- Sign-ups
- Downloads
- Sales

**Efficiency** :
- Cost per impression
- Cost per click
- Cost per conversion
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)
