# Advertising Workflow

## Overview

Advertisements are paid placements designed to reach specific audience and drive conversion (clicks, sign-ups, purchases).

---

## Process

1. **Analyze Audience** → Who are we targeting? What motivates them?
2. **Identify Problem** → What pain point are we addressing?
3. **Define Creative Angle** → AUTHORITY / OPPORTUNITY / HUMAN / NAVIGATION / DATA / EDUCATION?
4. **Craft Headline** → Attention-grabbing, benefit-focused
5. **Design Hook** → Visual that stops the scroll
6. **Develop Value Prop** → Why should they click/convert?
7. **Build Variations** → 2-4 variations (different angles, copy)
8. **Integrate CTA** → Clear, action-specific button or link
9. **Test** → A/B test variations, measure performance
10. **Optimize** → Iterate based on performance data
11. **Scale** → Increase budget for top-performing variation

---

## Platform-Specific Considerations

**Meta Ads (Facebook, Instagram)** :
- 1:1 or 4:5 format
- Text overlay limit (20% of image)
- Headline 25 characters, Description 30 characters
- CTA button placement

**LinkedIn** :
- Branded photo or company video
- Professional tone
- B2B messaging
- Thought leadership angle preferred

**Google Ads** :
- Search: Text-based headlines + descriptions
- Display: Image ads with clear CTA
- YouTube: Video pre-roll or bumper ads

---

## Key Decisions

- **Audience** : Who are we reaching?
- **Placement** : Which platform, which format?
- **Angle** : Which creative territory (6 angles)?
- **Hook** : What visual stops the scroll?
- **CTA** : Sign up? Download? Buy? Learn more?
- **Budget** : How much to spend testing?

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Headline is benefit-focused and specific
- [ ] Visual is eye-catching
- [ ] CTA is clear and action-oriented
- [ ] Tone matches audience
- [ ] Brand compliance verified
- [ ] Contrast meets accessibility standard
- [ ] No misleading claims (factually accurate)
- [ ] Text overlay <20% of image (for Meta ads)

---

## Performance Metrics to Track

- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Conversion rate (signups/purchases)
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
