# Social Media Workflow

## Overview

Social media creatives are optimized for specific platforms and designed for rapid consumption in a feed.

---

## Process

1. **Select Platform** → Instagram? LinkedIn? TikTok? Facebook?
2. **Know Platform Specs** → Dimensions, safe zones, audience behavior
3. **Determine Format** → Feed post? Story? Reel? Carousel?\n4. **Craft Hook** → First element must stop the scroll (1 second)
5. **Write Copy** → Engaging caption, optimal length for platform
6. **Design Visual** → Platform-specific format, bold and readable at small size
7. **Add CTA** → Action in caption or image (platform-dependent)
8. **Test on Platform** → Preview how it looks at actual size
9. **Optimize** → Adjust text size, crop, colors based on preview
10. **Schedule/Post** → Optimal posting time for audience
11. **Monitor** → Track engagement, respond to comments
12. **Iterate** → Learn what resonates, improve next posts

---

## Format-Specific Steps

**Feed Post (1:1, 4:5)** :
- Design for thumbnail visibility (200px width test)
- Headline minimum 28px
- CTA in caption (link in bio) or image

**Stories (9:16)** :
- Vertical full-frame
- Text overlay bold (48px+)
- CTA sticker or link sticker
- Ephemeral (24 hour lifespan)

**Reels (9:16 video)** :
- Hook in first 1 second (before audience scrolls)
- Fast-paced, energetic
- Captions overlaid on video
- 15-90 seconds duration

**Carousel (multiple slides)** :
- Slide 1: Hook (most important)
- Slides 2-3: Supporting info
- Each slide complete standalone
- Consistent aesthetic throughout

## Platform Optimization

| Platform | Posting Frequency | Best Time | Format | Tone |
|----------|-------------------|-----------|--------|------|
| Instagram | 3-4/week | 9 AM, 5-6 PM | Feed, Stories, Reels | Modern, engaging |
| LinkedIn | 2-3/week | Tue-Thu, 8 AM-2 PM | Feed, Document | Professional |
| Facebook | 2-3/week | 1-3 PM | Feed, Video | Community |
| TikTok | 2-4/week | Varies | Reels/videos | Authentic, trendy |

---

## Hashtag Strategy

**Instagram** : 5-10 relevant hashtags (mix popular + niche)

**LinkedIn** : 3-5 industry hashtags

**TikTok** : 3-5 trending hashtags

**Facebook** : 1-3 hashtags (less important)

---

## Engagement Strategy

1. Post at optimal time
2. Respond to comments within 1 hour
3. Engage with audience comments (like, reply)
4. Use 1-2 calls-to-action per post
5. Track which posts get most engagement
6. Repurpose top-performing content

---

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Dimensions correct for platform
- [ ] Text readable at thumbnail size (200px width)
- [ ] Hook captures attention (1 second test)
- [ ] CTA clear and actionable
- [ ] Image high-quality
- [ ] Captions engaging and benefit-focused
- [ ] Hashtags relevant and optimal count
- [ ] Brand compliance verified
- [ ] Tested on actual platform before posting
